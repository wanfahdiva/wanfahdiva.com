# Editorial motion

References applied from the UI Skills catalog:

- https://www.ui-skills.com/skills/jakubkrehel/better-ui
- https://www.ui-skills.com/skills/wshobson/interaction-design

Hero semantic groups stagger by 100ms. About reveals semantic groups once when they enter the viewport; offscreen content remains readable without the animation API. Interactive hover states use transitions, pointer capability gates, and existing press feedback. Play/pause icons cross-fade without changing button dimensions.

The splash and handoff use the shared `INTRO_TIMING` values: a 100ms lead-in, a 2800ms linear outline, and a 450ms fill beginning at 2750ms. The last 150ms of the outline overlaps the fill. The landing mounts as the fill finishes at 3200ms, and the splash fades for 850ms. Hero groups begin 150–450ms into that handoff; the scene begins at 250ms. There are no stationary holds between stages. Escape skips the splash. Reduced motion skips the intro and removes translation, scale and blur from these reveals.

About and Project headings use GSAP ScrollTrigger at `top 60%`, with one reveal per semantic group; no new runtime dependency, scroll listeners, word splitting, parallax, or looping decoration was added. GSAP contexts, matchMedia handlers and ScrollTriggers are reverted on cleanup. Keyboard focus cancels an in-progress reveal around the focused content.

Project previews share a fixed 16:9 viewport with cover positioning from the top-left. A 1.4% right-edge overscan hides scrollbars captured in the source screenshots. Original files remain unchanged. Preview width is capped against the card's available height, leaving a dedicated footer area.

Workbench turning: on hover (fine pointers only) the whole machine turns about the vertical axis through the middle of its deck, from azimuth 48° to 68° so the front plate faces the viewer. Turning about a vertical axis is the kit's own drawing at another azimuth (the light is camera-locked), so `workbench-scene.mjs` rebuilds the scene per whole degree with the same kit and the page swaps the layers in place. The authored paint order rests on separating planes along x, y and z, which holds for every azimuth strictly between 0° and 90°. The scene builder is a lazy ~5 KB gzip chunk loaded on first hover; frames are memoised and warmed in idle time. Touch and reduced motion stay at 48°.

Exploded view: press and hold the drawing (or hold Space/Enter while it is focused) to lift the placed sheets apart. Rows rise as a cascade (front cards, then text and image, then the header), drift outward from the canvas centre, and dashed slot outlines and leader lines tie each sheet to its place. Sheets still on the stack fan upward; the vacuum tool retracts into its housing so nothing lifted can reach it. Opening uses a slightly underdamped spring; closing is critically damped so sheets never sink through the canvas. Holding pauses assembly; release resumes only after every offset is back to zero. Touch waits 120 ms so a scroll cancels before anything opens. Reduced motion opens and closes instantly. The loop stops when settled, offscreen, or in a hidden tab. One quiet hint ("Hold to open the layers") replaces the earlier tooltips.

Book-like scroll layer. The hero leaves like a cover being turned: one scrubbed ScrollTrigger fades the copy and lifts it 18% while the machine drifts 22% slower than the page and dims to 35% (transform and opacity only, `use-hero-exit.ts`). Project screenshots and the About portrait are painted in once by `PaintReveal`: six ragged brush sweeps erase a paper overlay (SVG mask, ~1.1 s), the portrait on the same 60% reading line as the editorial reveal; the overlay is removed from the DOM once painted and anything already on screen at load is never hidden. A running folio (`folio.tsx`) sits vertically in the left gutter and names the chapter at the middle of the viewport via one IntersectionObserver; it is portalled to `<body>` because the page wrapper's filter would otherwise trap `position: fixed`. Toolkit rows fill with ink from the left on hover and invert their type; the ink leaves to the right. Reduced motion removes the exit scrub, paint and folio slide.

Mobile menu. The overlay's top row mirrors the header exactly (same gutter, 100/80 px height following the header's scrolled state, same logo ink), so the logo stays put and the close button replaces the menu button in place. Opening paints the page over with seven brush sweeps (~620 ms) before the contents arrive; closing runs it in reverse: contents leave, then the paper is scratched off bottom to top (640 ms) before the dialog closes. Opened by tap or click, focus moves to the dialog itself so no keyboard ring is drawn; opened by keyboard, focus lands on the close button. Reduced motion opens and closes on a solid sheet.
